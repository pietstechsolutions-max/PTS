How it works

One engine. Your name on it.

Every Pietvue app is the same shell, tuned to one trade. That is why a custom app costs what it costs — and why it is running in weeks, not next year.

1. We map your day

What your staff does at 6 AM, what your customer keeps calling about, what paperwork blocks the work. That becomes the home screen.

2. Three doors, one system

A staff app, a customer portal and a public page — all reading the same data, so nobody retypes anything or sees anyone else’s account.

3. Wired to the building

Cameras, gates, Wi-Fi and door access are already ours. The app is the screen on top of the system we installed.

White-labeled to you

Your logo, your colors, your domain. Customers never see the word Piets unless you want them to.

Supported 24/7

One number, one person who knows your system. No ticket queue, no offshore first line.

Your data stays yours

Export it whenever you want. No required cloud subscription to look at your own cameras or records.
