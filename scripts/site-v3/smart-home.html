Smart Home FAQ

What smart home hub do you use?

Open-source software that runs your smart home locally: lights, locks, thermostats, shades and cameras in one app, with no required subscription.

Do I need a monthly subscription?

No. It runs locally with no required cloud fees.

Will it work with my cameras?

Yes. We integrate your camera system so you can see who is at the door in the same app.
