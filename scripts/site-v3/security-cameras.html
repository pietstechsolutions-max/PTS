How we lay out a typical house

Most homes start with five cameras at the same height, tucked under the soffits: two front corners crossing each other, two rear corners crossing each other, and one side camera looking toward the front. Then we adjust for your driveway, doors, gates and blind spots. Want to see it first? Try the free Coverage Planner or put cameras on a photo of your house in the Design Studio.

Stores, restaurants and offices

For businesses we map every entry, register, stock room and parking area, and plan cameras together with access control and the network they run on. Registers and back doors get clear angles; lots get cameras built for low light. See commercial projects.

FAQ

Security Cameras FAQ

What camera brand do you install?

We are an InVid Tech Paramont authorized installer: IP cameras and recorders with 4K options, night vision and viewing from your phone, with no required monthly cloud fees.

How many cameras does my house need?

Most Long Island homes start around five: both front corners, both rear corners and one side. Bigger lots, detached garages and pools add more. Our guide How many security cameras does a Long Island home need? goes deeper.

Wired or wireless cameras?

Wired. Each camera gets one cable that carries power and data, so there are no batteries to charge and no Wi-Fi dropouts.

Do I need a permit for cameras in Suffolk County?

Usually not for cameras on your own home. Our guide Do you need a permit for security cameras in Suffolk County? covers when you might, plus the privacy rules to know.

Can you take over cameras another company installed?

Yes. If you can’t view or manage a system someone else put in, we diagnose it first and, when it makes sense, replace it with a Paramont system.

How much does it cost?

Every job is quoted to your site. There are no one-size packages. Book a free demo and you get a tailored plan and quote.
