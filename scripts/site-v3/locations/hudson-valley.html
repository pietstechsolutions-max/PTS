FAQ

Hudson Valley FAQ

Do you cover Westchester and Putnam County?

Yes. Westchester, Putnam, Dutchess and Orange County are all in our on-site service area.

Can you get Wi-Fi to a detached garage or barn?

Yes. Depending on distance we run fiber, direct-burial Cat6 or a wireless point-to-point bridge, then add an access point in the outbuilding.

Do you do smart home installs in the Hudson Valley?

Yes. Home Assistant automation for lighting, shades, thermostats, locks, cameras and gates, running locally on your own hardware with no required subscriptions.

What about long driveways and gates?

InVid Tech Paramont cameras with night vision, plus gate intercoms and access control you manage from your phone.
