FAQ

Straight answers

Is the demo really free?

Yes. You see your demo before you pay anything. If you don’t want it, you owe nothing.

What happens after I pay the deposit?

We call you, confirm the details, swap in your real photos and reviews, connect your domain and make it live. Starter sites are usually live in about a week.

Can my website be in another language?

Yes. Pick English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Italian or Polish when you build your demo. Need another language, or two languages on one site? Ask us and we will build it.

Do you work outside the US?

Yes. Websites are built and run online, so we work with businesses anywhere. Phone numbers, areas and language are set up for your country.

Do I own my domain?

Yes. Your domain stays in your name. We can help you buy one or move the one you have.

What does the monthly fee cover?

Hosting, SSL, security updates, backups and your plan’s monthly edits and features. No long contract.

Will you write fake reviews?

Never. We pull in your real Google reviews and help you get more.

What is The Whole Shabang?

Everything we do, all in, with constant hands-on support. We build the exact list around your business on a call.
