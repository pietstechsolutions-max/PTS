Live console

This is the whole thing. Click anything.

Move a job down the pipeline. Change a quantity in the quote and watch the tax, the total and the deposit schedule redo themselves. Then flip to the client side.

Sample data, running in your browser. Nothing is saved and nothing is sent. Real client names never appear in a Piets demo.

Why it exists

Most field businesses lose money in the gaps.

Not on the job — between the jobs. The quote that never got sent. The deposit nobody chased. The finished install that sat two weeks before anyone invoiced it.

Quote in minutes

Pick the lines, set the quantities. Pre-tax all the way down, tax once at the bottom, deposit schedule written for you — 50% plus tax up front, 25% on arrival, 25% on completion.

A pipeline that nags

Nothing sits in a stage without a next step and a date. Quote with no answer in three days? Field HQ sends the follow-up.

The calendar clients can see

Day-before reminder, on-my-way ping, arrival window. The “what time are you coming” call stops.

Photos that land somewhere

Shoot it on the phone, it attaches to the job and shows up in the client’s portal. No texting pictures to yourself.

Invoices, numbered and logged

Straight from the approved quote. Due on receipt, every payment method on it, nothing blank for anyone to fill in later.

One thread per client

Texts, approvals and questions all on the job, not scattered across three phones and an inbox.

Two sides, one truth

Your crew sees the work. Your client sees their job.

The team side

  • Today list, pipeline board and the week at a glance
  • Quote builder with your own price book behind it
  • Jobs, photos, materials and notes on one record
  • Invoice numbering and a payment log that balances
  • Works on the phone in the van, not just the office

The client side

  • A link you text them — no account, no password
  • Read and approve the quote by typing their name
  • Every visit, with a reminder the day before
  • Install photos they can keep
  • Invoices and a message thread straight to you
