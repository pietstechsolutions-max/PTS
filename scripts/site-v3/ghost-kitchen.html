The build

What actually goes into a ghost kitchen.

A delivery-only kitchen fails on logistics, not food. These are the things that go wrong in week one if nobody set them up properly.

Order tablets, tamed

Mounted, powered, labelled and on their own network segment — not five tablets on a power strip falling off the shelf.

Printers & KDS that route

Grill, fryer and expo each see only their own tickets. One screen per station, and a paper fallback when a screen dies mid-rush.

Wi-Fi that holds at 7pm

Wired where it can be wired, proper access points where it cannot, and a second internet path so one outage is not a closed night.

Cameras on the handoff

Line of sight on the pickup shelf and the back door. This is what wins a “never arrived” dispute, and it is why the cameras matter here.

Door access for drivers

Drivers get in to the pickup area and nowhere else, with a log of who opened what and when.

Your own ordering page

Built by Piets, on your own domain, with your own customer list behind it. Built from the same engine as our website demos.

The dispute desk

“It never arrived.” Prove otherwise.

Refunds and chargebacks off the ordering and delivery platforms are a straight hit to a kitchen’s margin, and most operators eat them because gathering the evidence weeks later is not worth the hour. So collect it at the time, automatically.

The dispute desk is the third tab of the console above — jump back up and open “Chargebacks & disputes”.

1. Order record

Itemised, timestamped, tied to the channel it came from.

2. Handoff footage

The clip from the pickup door at the moment on the ticket, kept long enough to matter.

3. Driver trail

Assignment, pickup scan and the GPS drop pin from the delivery platform.

4. The packet

Assembled in the format the platform or the card network asks for, filed inside the response window.

An honest word about win rates. Nobody can promise you a number, and anyone who does is selling. The platforms and the card networks decide, their rules change, and some disputes are lost whatever you file. What is in your control is turning up with the evidence every single time instead of some of the time — and that part Piets can genuinely automate.

Also worth saying

Run both. Just know what each one costs you.

The marketplaces bring you customers you would never have met. Your own channel keeps the ones you already have. The mistake is not picking one — it is never working out the difference. Put your own commission rate and your own food cost into “What platforms cost” in the console above and it will split one sample ticket into food, commission and what is actually left.
