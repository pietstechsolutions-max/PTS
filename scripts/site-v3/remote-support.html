The session, before you have one

Press Decline and see what happens. Nothing. That is the point.

Everyone has heard about the remote-access scam, so here is the whole thing laid out before you ever pick up the phone. Both screens below are drawn in code with sample files on them — no real computer, no real ID, nothing to install to try it.

The ID comes from you, out loud. RustDesk shows an ID on your screen. We cannot look it up, guess it or reach your machine without it — and even with it, nothing opens until you accept the box on your own monitor.

Close RustDesk at any moment and the session is over. There is nothing left running afterwards and no way back in.

If someone rings you first, that is not us. Piets does not cold-call about a virus, a refund or an expiring warranty. Hang up, then call 631-871-5957 yourself — not a number you were just given. The “Make sure it is really us” tab above is the short checklist.
