    FAQ

    Straight answers

    Do I have to open anything on my router?

    No. The box calls out to the Piets Hub over an encrypted line. Nothing on your router is opened to the internet.

    Can Piets see my private stuff?

    The box watches the health of your network and devices: online, offline, speed, updates. Remote help on a computer only happens when you say OK.

    What if my internet goes down?

    Add backup internet and the box switches to a backup line so cards and cameras keep working. Without backup, we still see the drop and can call your provider for you.

    What does it cost?

    Ask us. Every setup is tailored to your place, and we give you a clear price before anything is installed.

    Is it only for businesses?

    No. Homes get the same watch, plus a smart home hub for lights, locks and cameras.

    Can it run my TV?

    Yes. TV screen mode shows welcome screens, menu boards or slides that Piets updates remotely.
