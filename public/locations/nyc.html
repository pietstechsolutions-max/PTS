FAQ

New York City FAQ

Do you install cameras in NYC apartment buildings?

Yes. InVid Tech Paramont cameras and recorders for lobbies, hallways, entrances, roofs and basements, plus video intercoms and access control so residents and managers can see and control entry from a phone.

Can you work around building management requirements?

Yes. We provide certificates of insurance, coordinate with supers and management, and schedule around building rules and quiet hours.

Do you support NYC restaurants and ghost kitchens?

Yes. POS, kitchen printers, order tablets, menu boards, cameras and reliable Wi-Fi. We set up delivery-only kitchens to take orders from day one.

Is remote support available in NYC?

Yes. Many software, email, printer and network issues can be handled remotely with RustDesk.
