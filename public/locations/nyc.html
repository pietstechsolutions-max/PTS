FAQ

New York City FAQ

Do you install cameras in NYC apartment buildings?

Yes. Paramont cameras and NVRs for lobbies, hallways, entrances, roofs and basements, plus video intercoms and access control so residents and managers can see and control entry from a phone.

Can you work around building management requirements?

Yes. We provide certificates of insurance, coordinate with supers and management, and schedule around building rules and quiet hours.

Do you support NYC restaurants and ghost kitchens?

Yes. POS, kitchen printers, order tablets, menu boards, cameras and reliable Wi-Fi. We’ve set up delivery-only kitchens that need to be taking orders on day one.

Is remote support available in NYC?

Yes. Most software, email, printer and network issues are solved remotely via RustDesk in under an hour, so you don’t wait for a truck.
