FAQ

Long Island FAQ

How fast can you get to me on Long Island?

Same-day or next-day in most of Suffolk County and western Nassau. Emergencies (cameras down, network outage, POS not working) get priority any day of the week.

Do you install cameras in Suffolk County homes?

Yes. Most Long Island homes need 4 to 8 InVid Tech Paramont cameras covering the driveway, front door, backyard and side yards. We’ll walk the property and show you a live demo first.

Can you fix Wi-Fi in a large Long Island house?

That’s one of our most common jobs. We survey the house, wire access points where they belong and eliminate dead zones in the basement, upstairs bedrooms and the backyard.

Do you support restaurants and delis?

Absolutely. POS, kitchen printers, TV menu boards, cameras, phones and Wi-Fi for staff and guests. We understand you can’t be down during a rush.
