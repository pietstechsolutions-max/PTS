FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do you really answer 24/7?

Yes — call or text 631-871-5957 any time. Questions? We're here 24/7.

Is the demo really free?

Yes. We can walk you through a camera system, Wi-Fi upgrade or POS setup in person or on a video call, with no obligation. References are available on request.

Which areas do you cover?

Suffolk and Nassau County on Long Island, all five NYC boroughs, the Hudson Valley (Westchester, Putnam, Dutchess, Orange) and the Johnstown / Capital Region. Remote support is available anywhere, and larger commercial projects anywhere in the US on request.

What camera brand do you install?

We are an InVid Tech Paramont authorized installer: IP cameras and recorders with 4K options, night vision and viewing from your phone — with no required monthly cloud fees.

Do I need a monthly plan?

No. Managed plans are optional — no contract is required to get service. They are there for people who want remote monitoring, camera health checks and priority support.

How much does it cost?

Every job is quoted to your site — there are no one-size packages. Book a free demo and you get a tailored plan and quote.

What payment methods do you accept?

Zelle, Venmo, Cash App, cash or check at no extra charge; credit/debit card via a secure payment link (4% card processing fee).

How does remote support work?

We use RustDesk. You open RustDesk, read us your ID, and we connect with your permission.
